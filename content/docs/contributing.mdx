## Community

Have questions, feedback, or want to join the discussion? Connect with us on [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/OpenBangla/OpenBangla-Keyboard/discussions) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/openbanglakeyboard).

## Contributing

We welcome contributions! See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/OpenBangla/OpenBangla-Keyboard/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) for full details on the contribution process.

If you find a bug or have a feature request, please [open an issue](https://github.com/OpenBangla/OpenBangla-Keyboard/issues).
